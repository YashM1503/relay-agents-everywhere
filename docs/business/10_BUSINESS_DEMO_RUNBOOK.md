# Business / Demo Lead Runbook

## Mission
Own the reason this matters. Builders should not spend time on slides, fake data, or market storytelling.

## Deliverables
1. final user/problem statement
2. synthetic demo dataset
3. 6–7 slide pitch
4. 2-minute demo script
5. “why this hackathon” narrative
6. business case / buyer
7. competitor/prior-art notes
8. submission description + social post draft

## Framing
Do not say:
- “AI for seniors”
- “AI monitors vulnerable people”
- “universal super-assistant”
- “always-on AI”

Say:
> RELAY is a phone-first accessibility and action layer for people who need technology to adapt to them, not the other way around.

## Buyer story
End user:
- person completing a difficult workflow

Economic buyer:
- clinic / government service / insurer / bank / transit / service organization

Buyer value:
- fewer abandoned workflows
- fewer support calls
- less staff hand-holding
- better accessibility
- clearer consent/audit trail

## Business hypotheses
Present as hypotheses:
- 20%+ reduction in abandonment on selected difficult workflows
- 5–10 minutes less staff assistance per difficult transaction
- 90%+ successful completion on narrow workflow
- near-zero unconfirmed consequential submissions
- high user comprehension

## Demo dataset
Create/validate:
- user profile
- fake clinic form
- fake insurance card
- appointment slots
- deliberate error
- confirmation code
- optional suspicious message
- agent registry
- action policy

Synthetic data only.

## Pitch deck outline
1. Problem
2. Insight — phone is where digital and physical life meet
3. Product — Talk / Show / Stay with me / Watch
4. Demo
5. Architecture — agent-agnostic router + COUNTERSIGN
6. Business
7. Expansion — WhatsApp, wearables, IoT, buildings, mobility

## Two-minute demo
### 0–15 sec
“People are increasingly asked to scan, upload, switch apps, remember steps, and navigate interfaces during real-world interactions. RELAY lets the phone help without forcing the person into another chatbot.”

### 15–35 sec
Clerk:
“Scan this QR code, complete registration, upload your insurance card, and book an appointment.”

User:
“RELAY, stay with me.”

### 35–75 sec
RELAY understands task, opens workflow, says:
“I already know 12 of 18 fields. I need six things from you.”
User answers.
Camera captures card.
RELAY handles form error.

### 75–100 sec
RELAY:
“Everything is ready. This will send your contact information and insurance card to Demo Clinic. Submit?”

User: “Yes.”

### 100–115 sec
Submit + confirmation + calendar.

### 115–120 sec
“RELAY is agent-agnostic underneath and uses COUNTERSIGN before consequential actions. Technology should adapt to the person, not the other way around.”
